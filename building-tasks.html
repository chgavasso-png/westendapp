Building Task Descriptions

The following document outlines the standard cleaning procedures and task allocations for the respective segments and blocks within the main building property. These tasks are structured into Daily, Weekly, and Monthly frequencies to ensure the highest standard of hygiene and maintenance.

🏢 Skyline

📅 Daily

  • Vacuum and dusting
  • Glasses, mirrors and touchpoints
  • Lift floors and doors
  • Mopping hard floor
  • Bin empty reception area
  • B1 & B2 general cleaning
  • Floors general cleaning includes dusting, vacuuming, skirting, marking on the walls, and lift doors smear-free
  • Bin shut areas mopped and dusted
  • Staircase general cleaning (dust removal in fitting, skirting boards, handrails, and vacuum floors. Mop as necessary)
  • Bin replacements in B2 twice a day (general and recycling bins)
  • Sweep and clean carpark area
  • Scrubbed hard floors, jet wash corners as necessary outside areas
  • Clean management office (toilets, kitchen, reception, office, staircase)
  • Welfare area (toilet, showers, changing room, lunch area, corridors)

🗓️ Weekly

  • Rubbish collection from the basement twice a week
  • Bin store clean and sanitised B2
  • Carpark floor scrubbed clean once
  • Wiped carpark doors and fittings B2
  • Window cleaning of main entrances
  • Podium garden sweep and litter picking x2

📆 Monthly

  • Apply algin killer on external as necessary
  • Clean plant pots
  • Clean drains as necessary

📅 Daily

  • Sweeping all external areas

  • Window cleaning all glasses
