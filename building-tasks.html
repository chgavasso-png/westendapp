Building Task Descriptions

The following document outlines the standard cleaning procedures and task allocations for the respective segments and blocks within the main building property. These tasks are structured to ensure the highest standard of hygiene and maintenance.

🏢 Skyline

  • Vacuum and dusting
  • Glasses, mirrors and touchpoints
  • Lift floors and doors
  • Mopping hard floor
  • Bin empty reception area
  • B1 & B2 general cleaning
  • Floors general cleaning (dusting, vacuuming, skirting, marks on walls, lift doors smear-free)
  • Bin shut areas mopped and dusted
  • Staircase general cleaning & vacuum floors
  • Bin replacements in B2 twice a day (general and recycling)
  • Sweep and clean carpark area
  • Scrubbed hard floors, jet wash corners outside
  • Clean management office (toilets, kitchen, reception, office, staircase)
  • Welfare area (toilet, showers, changing room, lunch area, corridors)
  • Rubbish collection from the basement twice a week
  • Bin store clean and sanitised B2
  • Carpark floor scrubbed clean once
  • Wiped carpark doors and fittings B2
  • Window cleaning of main entrances
  • Podium garden sweep and litter picking x2
  • Apply algae killer on external as necessary
  • Clean plant pots & drains as necessary

🏪 Commercial Units

  • Frames and walls to be wiped free of dust

  • Deep clean front of shops floors
